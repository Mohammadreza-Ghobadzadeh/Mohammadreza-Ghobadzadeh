# Mohammadreza Ghobadzadeh

Control systems researcher working at the intersection of **optimal control, model predictive control, distributed optimization, energy systems, and learning-based control**.

M.Sc. in Control Systems Engineering, University of Tehran  
Research Assistant — Smart Network Laboratory & Advanced Control Systems Laboratory (ACSL)

My work connects control theory with numerical optimization and practical implementation in MATLAB, Python, CasADi, and CVXPY.

## Research Interests

* Control Theory and Optimization-Based Control
* Model Predictive Control and Learning-Based MPC
* Distributed Optimization and Multi-Agent Systems
* Photovoltaics and Solar Cell Optimization
* Power and Energy Systems
* Reinforcement Learning and Robotics

## Featured Educational Project

### [Workshop Materials](https://github.com/Mohammadreza-Ghobadzadeh/Workshop-Materials)

A single public collection of three Persian-language university workshops, with session notes, lecture links, and source codes in one repository:

* **Numerical Optimal Control** — CasADi/MATLAB, direct and indirect methods, shooting, DP, LQR
* **Model Predictive Control using CasADi** — receding-horizon control and nonlinear MPC
* **Distributed Optimization** — CVXPY notebooks plus complementary CasADi examples

Lectures: [Control Research Team on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Control_Research_Team)

## Research Snapshot

* Learning-based MPC and predictive safety filters for systems with uncertain dynamics
* Distributed MPC for cooperative multi-vessel ship towing
* Multi-agent optimal-control reconstruction of NGSIM lane-change maneuvers
* Prediction-based adaptive sliding-mode control under input delay
* Coalition-based and uncertainty-aware energy management for smart homes and microgrids
* Fabrication-tolerant design of all-perovskite tandem solar cells

Selected publication: *Beyond Exact Current Matching* — Solar RRL, [DOI: 10.1002/solr.70434](https://doi.org/10.1002/solr.70434)
